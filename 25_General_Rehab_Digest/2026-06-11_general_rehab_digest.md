### 1. ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #1 · ⭐ HIGH — Two-Week Interdisciplinary Inpatient Rehab Produces Significant Functional and Speech Gains in Parkinson's Disease

PMC [PMC12872888](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12872888/)
*Frontiers in Rehabilitation Sciences* · 2026 | GR07

**Key outcomes:** AM-PAC Basic Mobility and Daily Activity improved significantly (p<0.0001) from admission to discharge; Section GG self-care and mobility scores improved (p<0.001); Voice Handicap Index improved significantly (p=0.028). No adverse events reported in this elective program.
**Methods:** Prospective single-center study. N=37 patients with Parkinson's disease admitted to a 2-week elective acute inpatient multidisciplinary rehabilitation program at JFK Johnson Rehabilitation Institute (New Jersey). Assessments: AM-PAC, Section GG IRF-PAI, VHI.
**Rehab take:** Two-week elective inpatient rehabilitation for PD is safe and produces meaningful functional and voice outcomes measurable on standard IRF assessment tools (AM-PAC, Section GG, VHI). Directly supports IRF admission criteria justification and intake goal-setting.
**Team take:**
- 🦿 **PT** · Daily PT intensity with cueing strategies and gait training drove AM-PAC Basic Mobility gains; cueing-based gait training and fall prevention protocols are the PT core.
- ✋ **OT** · AM-PAC Daily Activity gains confirm ADL training effectiveness; focus on fine motor tasks, instrumental ADLs, and home modification planning within the 2-week window.
- 🗣️ **SLP** · Statistically significant VHI improvement is a standout finding; LSVT LOUD or phonatory intensity exercises within a 2-week IRF window can produce measurable voice outcomes.
- 🩺 **Nursing** · Dopaminergic medication timing optimization before therapy sessions and Parkinson's-specific fall prevention protocols are essential nursing contributions to these outcomes.
- 🧠 **Psychology** · Depression and apathy are common in PD and directly affect therapy participation; brief psychiatric screening on admission is warranted.

### 2. ⭐ HIGH — Standardized Early Weight-Bearing Starting 2–3 Days Post-Op Improves Hip Function, FIM, and QOL in Intertrochanteric Fractures — RCT

PMC [PMC13104271](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13104271/)
*BMC Geriatrics* · 2026 | GR02 · GR11

**Key outcomes:** Early weight-bearing group achieved significantly better Harris Hip Score, lower VAS pain, higher FIM scores, and better QOL at discharge and 1-month follow-up vs. conventional 4-week delayed weight-bearing. Exercise adherence was higher in the early group.
**Methods:** RCT. N=54 older adults (27 intervention / 27 control) post-surgical fixation for intertrochanteric femoral fracture. Intervention: standardized early WB program starting day 2–3 postop. Control: routine rehab with WB initiation at 4 weeks. Outcomes: Harris Hip Score, VAS pain, FIM, QOL, exercise adherence.
**Rehab take:** Weight-bearing precautions for intertrochanteric fractures should be reviewed with orthopedic surgery on each admission. Early WB (day 2–3) following intramedullary fixation appears safe and superior to 4-week delay in older adults. Consider protocol revision for WBAT orders at IRF admission for stable fixation.
**Team take:**
- 🦿 **PT** · Early WB protocol driver; progressive ambulation from day 2–3 post-op using assistive devices; gait training frequency correlated with FIM improvement.
- ✋ **OT** · Earlier mobilization enables earlier ADL engagement; bed-to-chair, toilet transfers, and LE dressing achievable sooner with early WB status.
- 🩺 **Nursing** · Fall prevention protocols must accompany early WB orders; pain management optimization timing (pre-therapy analgesia) matters for participation.
- 🧠 **Psychology** · Fear of falling and pain catastrophizing are common barriers to early WB adherence; brief motivational support improves compliance.

### 3. ⭐ HIGH — Brain-Computer Interface With FES and Virtual Reality Targets Gait in Multiple Sclerosis: Pilot Trial Shows Sustained Motor Gains to Week 37

PMC [PMC12812722](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12812722/)
*Frontiers in Medicine* · 2026 | GR05

**Key outcomes:** 24 of 26 participants completed 30 BCI sessions. Gait and motor outcomes improved at week 13 with partial maintenance at weeks 17 and 37 follow-up. All MS subtypes included (RRMS 37.5%, PPMS 33.3%, SPMS 16.7%).
**Methods:** Pilot single-arm intervention trial. N=26 persons with MS (24 completers), mean age 54, median 17.4 years since diagnosis. Intervention: motor-imagery BCI + functional electrical stimulation + virtual reality avatar targeting gait. 30 sessions over 13 weeks.
**Rehab take:** BCI-FES-VR represents an emerging neuroplasticity-targeting technology for motor rehabilitation in MS. Not yet standard-of-care, but progressive MS subtypes showed engagement and durable improvement. Relevant for program development planning.
**Team take:**
- 🦿 **PT** · BCI-driven gait rehab adds neuroplasticity mechanisms to standard PT; motor imagery protocols and FES augmentation are a growing evidence edge for MS PT practice.
- ✋ **OT** · BCI interfaces require upper extremity control and cognitive engagement; OT assessment of hand function and processing speed may be a prerequisite for BCI program candidacy.
- 🧠 **Psychology** · Technology acceptance, motivation, and belief in motor imagery effectiveness influence BCI engagement; psychoeducation may improve adherence and outcomes.

### 4. MEDIUM — Worse Baseline Function Predicts Greater IRF Gains After TKA — Ceiling Effects Limit Benefits for Best-Functioning Patients

PMID [39849999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39849999/)
*Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine* · 2025 | GR01

**Key outcomes:** Patients in the lowest baseline quartile (worst function) showed the greatest absolute improvements in WOMAC, HAQ, EQ-5D-5L, ROM, and TUG during 3-week IRF. Highest-functioning quartile showed ceiling effects — minimal change observed.
**Methods:** Retrospective observational study. N=329 patients (63.8% female, mean age 68.25) undergoing 3-week inpatient rehabilitation following TKA in Austria. Assessed at admission and discharge with NPRS, HAQ, EQ-5D-5L, WOMAC, ROM, TUG. Patients divided into quartile groups by baseline score.
**Rehab take:** IRF admission for TKA is most impactful for patients with poor baseline function. Frame IRF goals and LOS justification around baseline quartile performance — patients with low preoperative function benefit most from intensive inpatient rehabilitation.
**Team take:**
- 🦿 **PT** · ROM and TUG showed the most objective improvement in the lowest baseline quartile; tailor early goal-setting to starting ROM and gait status rather than applying fixed protocols.
- ✋ **OT** · HAQ score improvements were significant in the lowest baseline group; OT should prioritize patients with the greatest functional limitation on admission for most intensive ADL intervention.

### 6. MEDIUM — SICOT 3-Stage Polytrauma Rehabilitation Framework: Acute Stabilization → Functional Restoration → Community Reintegration — Systematic Review

PMID [39680082](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39680082/)
*International Orthopaedics* · 2025 | GR03

**Key outcomes:** 5,212 studies screened; 7 included. Three-stage framework: (1) acute stabilization and early mobilization; (2) intensive functional restoration (IRF scope); (3) community reintegration. Structured recommendations developed for each stage.
**Methods:** Systematic literature review with clinical recommendations on behalf of SICOT (Société Internationale de Chirurgie Orthopédique et de Traumatologie). Covers acute, post-acute, and long-term rehabilitation stages.
**Rehab take:** Use this 3-stage framework to structure IRF polytrauma admission goals. Stage 2 (intensive functional restoration) maps directly to IRF scope. Explicit Stage 3 community reintegration goals should be embedded in discharge planning from day one of admission.
**Team take:**
- 🦿 **PT** · Stages 2–3 feature gait restoration, progressive strengthening, and return-to-work capacity; community ambulation goals should be set at IRF admission.
- 🩺 **Nursing** · Stage 1 nursing priorities (pressure ulcer prevention, DVT prophylaxis, nutritional support) directly inform IRF admission nursing care plans for polytrauma.

### 8. MEDIUM — Microsurgical Flap Reconstruction Resolves Post-Burn Joint Contracture in ~99% of Pedicled Cases — Systematic Review of 830 Joints

PMC [PMC12357132](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12357132/)
*Microsurgery* · 2025 | GR04

**Key outcomes:** 27 studies included; 830 joint contractures reconstructed. Contracture resolution: 98.9% pedicled flaps, 90.1% free flaps. Recurrence rate: 1.8%. Flap non-viability: 1.5% pedicled, 2.9% free.
**Methods:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA). Searched PubMed, EMBASE, Scopus, Web of Science. Included studies describing flap-based reconstruction of post-burn joint contractures.
**Rehab take:** Understanding surgical reconstruction options for burn contractures informs IRF discharge planning. Patients with significant contractures limiting function may require staged surgical intervention post-IRF; surgical timelines affect rehabilitation goal-setting.
**Team take:**
- 🦿 **PT** · Conservative stretching and ROM work should be maximized before surgical candidacy assessment; post-reconstructive flap ROM protocols require careful progression.
- ✋ **OT** · Functional splinting, positioning, and adaptive ADL strategies are OT priorities when contracture impairs hand or upper limb function pending or following reconstruction.
- 🩺 **Nursing** · Signs of contracture progression should be documented and communicated to the burn surgical team during IRF; wound and graft monitoring is essential.

**Key outcomes:** Evidence supports: moderate tailored exercise (slows ALSFRS-R decline), early respiratory therapy (FVC monitoring, NIV timing), SLP for dysphagia/dysarthria/AAC, psychological/palliative care integration. Multidisciplinary coordination recommended at every disease stage.

### ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #1 — Two-Week Parkinson's IRF Produces Measurable Gains on Section GG, AM-PAC, and VHI

PMC [PMC12872888](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12872888/) · *Frontiers in Rehabilitation Sciences* · 2026 · GR07

> The first prospective US study to demonstrate that an elective 2-week acute inpatient multidisciplinary rehabilitation admission for Parkinson's disease produces statistically significant improvements on AM-PAC Basic Mobility, AM-PAC Daily Activity, Section GG self-care/mobility, and Voice Handicap Index — all standard IRF documentation tools. N=37. Zero adverse events. **For IDT rounds:** This paper directly supports PD IRF admission justification and provides benchmarks for achievable outcomes within a 2-week intensive inpatient program.

### ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #2 — PES Accelerates Post-Extubation Dysphagia Recovery in Acute Stroke — RCT Pilot With IRF Transfer Implications

PMC [PMC12418440](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12418440/) · *Neurotherapeutics* · 2025 · GR13

> RCT pilot (N=60): 3 days of early pharyngeal electrical stimulation post-extubation produced dramatically faster dysphagia recovery vs. sham — FOIS 4.1 vs 2.1 at day 3, reduced ICU and hospital LOS. **For SLP and Nursing at IDT rounds:** Patients transferred from acute stroke-extubation settings with PES treatment history may present with faster oral intake recovery trajectories. Inform diet upgrade protocols and FOIS progression expectations accordingly.

### ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #3 — ALS 2025 Clinical Practice Recommendations: A Full-IDT Framework Across All Disease Stages

PMC [PMC12693028](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12693028/) · *Journal of Clinical Medicine* · 2025 · GR06

> Comprehensive narrative review with clinical recommendations covering PT, OT, respiratory, SLP, psychology, and palliative care in ALS — published December 2025. Evidence supports: moderate tailored exercise (slows ALSFRS-R decline), early FVC monitoring, AAC planning before speech loss, PEG coordination with SLP assessment, and psychological support for patients and caregivers across all stages. **For IDT rounds:** Use as a practical reference for structuring multidisciplinary ALS admission goals. Recommend distributing to discipline team leads.
