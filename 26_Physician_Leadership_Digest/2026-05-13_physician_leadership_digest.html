1. CMS Just Quietly Cut Procedural Physician Pay Without Changing a Single Workday

CMS CY 2026 Medicare Physician Fee Schedule Final Rule (CMS-1832-F) — CMS Fact Sheet · November 2025 | PL09

Key outcomes: A 2.5% "efficiency adjustment" reduces work RVUs for nearly all non–time-based CPT codes. Affects ~7,000 physician services — 91% of all services. Time-based services (E&M, psychiatry, primary care) are exempt. Procedural/surgical/imaging specialists doing identical work generate fewer wRVUs, may miss thresholds, may trigger clawbacks — without changing clinical effort. Confirmed by PYA, MGMA, Nixon Peabody.

Methods: CMS Final Rule CMS-1832-F, effective January 1, 2026.

🔥 Hot take: Your administrator didn't cut your pay. CMS did — and your administrator may be counting on you not knowing the difference. If your hospital isn't "shadowing" 2026 wRVUs back to 2025 values, you are absorbing a stealth pay cut for doing the same job. Ask your CMO what the plan is. In writing. Before your next comp review.

2. 25 Studies. One Verdict: Prior Authorization Causes Measurable Patient Harm.

PMID 40912445 — American Journal of Medicine · 2026 Jan;139(1):24-32 (epub Sep 3, 2025) | PL07

Key outcomes: Systematic review of 25 U.S. studies across asthma, IBD, biologic therapies, psychiatric/SUD medications, anticoagulants, antibiotics, oncology. PA associated with: care delays, disease exacerbations, preventable hospitalizations, prolonged stays, and lower disease-free survival in cancer. In oncology, even 1–3 week delays correlate with worse disease control and lower survival.

Methods: Systematic review; 25 U.S. studies. Lead author: Jacob Murphy et al., Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine. DOI: 10.1016/j.amjmed.2025.08.018.

🔥 Hot take: The argument "prior auth just makes people wait a little" is now dead. Hopkins buried it with 25 studies. When the next denial lands, attach PMID 40912445 to your peer-to-peer request. The insurer is banking on you not citing it.

3. Physician Union Petitions Surged 11-Fold in 2023–2024. They're Winning 77% of Certifications.

PMID 39693063 — JAMA · January 2025 | PL08

Key outcomes: 77 union petitions 2000–2024. Annual rate: 2.1/year (2000–2022) → 23.3/year (2023–2024) — 11-fold increase. Certification: 62% overall; 77% in 2023–2024. 7,064 physicians total. Employers: 49% hospitals, 38% CHCs, 13% corporate. Motivations: working conditions (85%), lack of management voice (81%), patient care (54%).

Methods: Retrospective NLRB petition analysis. Authors: Rooke-Ley H, Richman BD, Bowling AB, Nikolov NJ, Schulman KD. JAMA, January 2025.

🔥 Hot take: Physicians filing at 11× the historical rate, winning 3 of 4 certifications. The top driver isn't money — it's autonomy and voice. Eliminating physician input while expecting professional loyalty may be building the next bargaining unit.
🌶️🌶️🌶️ HOT

4. Nonprofit Hospital CEOs Got 3× the Raise Workers Did Over 14 Years. The IRS Has the Receipts.

Pay Gap Between Nonprofit Hospital CEOs And Employees Grew, 2009–23 — Health Affairs · 2024 | PL02

Key outcomes: Analysis of 1,424 U.S. nonprofit hospitals. CEO-to-worker ratio: 10.2× (2009) → 12.0× (2023) — 17.6% widening. CEO pay: +27.5% ($814K → $1.04M). Worker pay: +9.8%. HCA CEO Sam Hazen: $23,799,137 (2024) vs. median HCA employee $60,820 — 391:1.

Methods: Longitudinal, 1,424 nonprofits, 2009–2023. IRS Form 990 + Medicare cost reports. U Chicago, Brown, RAND. DOI: 10.1377/hlthaff.2024.01494.

🔥 Hot take: Your hospital is a nonprofit. Its CEO's compensation is public record at ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer tonight. Find your CEO's Schedule C number, divide by median physician pay. Decide if you want that ratio in your next MEC meeting.

5. Physician CEO: OR 1.59 for Top Hospital Rankings. Only 22% of Hospitals Have One.

Leadership Practices, Organization Structure, and Other Factors Associated with Higher 2022–2023 US News and World Report Ranking — J Gen Intern Med · December 2025 | PL01

Key outcomes: Physician CEO: OR 1.59 (95% CI 1.27–2.00, P<0.001). For-profit: OR 0.44 (P=0.005). Only 22% of institutions (118/546) physician-led. Group practice: OR 1.25 (P=0.042). N=546 institutions, 1,676 hospitals.

Methods: Cross-sectional, 2022–2023 USNWR rankings. N=546 institutions, 1,676 hospitals. Multiple logistic regression. PMC12686240. DOI: 10.1007/s11606-024-09028-7.

🔥 Hot take: Physician-led hospitals have 59% better odds of top quality rankings. 78% of hospitals aren't physician-led. OR 1.59 is your one-slide argument for every board conversation about CMO authority. Bring the confidence intervals.

7. Private Equity Physicians: Barely Half as Likely to Stay, Significantly Less Satisfied.

PMID 38466275 — JAMA Internal Medicine · March 2024 | PL05

Key outcomes: N=529 physicians; 5.5% in PE practices. PE-employed: 44.8% likely to remain vs. 77.8% in non-PE settings. Significantly lower satisfaction and autonomy. BMJ 2023 SR (55 studies, 8 countries): PE "mixed to harmful" quality, higher costs. Hospital-physician consolidation: 17% higher commercial office-visit prices (GAO-25-107450).

🔥 Hot take: The physicians who took the PE deal are leaving at nearly twice the rate. Ask for 3-year retention data before you sign. If they can't produce it, that IS the answer.

🥊 Tactics this week

ISO Week 20 → Category 4: Get the CPE. It's the Credential Admin Actually Respects.

Why this matters: The JGIM data (item 5) show physician CEO leadership is associated with OR 1.59 for top hospital quality rankings — but only 22% of hospitals are physician-led. The CPE (Certified Physician Executive) is the formal signal that you are operating in leadership's domain on professional terms.

The concrete step: Visit aapl.org — the American Association for Physician Leadership. AAPL awards the CPE credential requiring AAPL-accredited education, a leadership assessment, and peer review portfolio. Cost: ~$5,000–$15,000 vs. $60,000–$120,000 for an MBA. Membership alone ($600–$800/year): Physician Leadership Journal, governance/negotiation white papers, national executive network, CME. For Jon's profile — physiatrist, SCI Program Director, MSU adjunct — CPE + existing leadership roles = documented pathway to CMO, VPMA, or governance board.

Time to do it: 15 minutes to explore aapl.org and join; 30 minutes to map your CPE pathway.
