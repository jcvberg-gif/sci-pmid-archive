## IM01 — Hypertension

### 1. MEDIUM — Inpatient Hypertension in Non-HTN Patients: No Universal Standard Exists
PMID [40656280](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40656280/)
*Cureus* · 2025 | IM01

**Key outcomes:** Systematic review of 12 studies (2015–2025) via PRISMA found no universal guideline for managing elevated BP in patients hospitalized for non-HTN reasons; existing evidence favors conservative non-pharmacological approaches; aggressive acute antihypertensive escalation in asymptomatic inpatients is not evidence-supported.
**Methods:** Systematic guideline review; Medline, PubMed, Embase, Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus; 12 studies met inclusion criteria; non-HTN hospitalized adult patients.
**Rehab take:** Reflexive BP dose-escalation for asymptomatic readings in IRF patients (SCI, stroke, hip fracture) is unsupported; home regimen resumption as tolerated — with attention to orthostatic hypotension common in SCI — is the appropriate default at MFB.

## IM02 — Diabetes

### 2. ⭐ HIGH — Diabetes Blunts Neurological Recovery in Older Cervical SCI Patients [TOP 3 #3]
PMID [39494742](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39494742/)
*Global Spine Journal* · 2025 | IM02

**Key outcomes:** Diabetic patients showed significantly worse neurological recovery vs. propensity-matched non-diabetic controls; effect most pronounced with moderate-to-severe DM; diabetes was an independent negative predictor of neurological improvement in elderly CSCI without bone injury.
**Methods:** Retrospective multicenter study; N=389 consecutive patients aged ≥65 with cervical SCI without bone injury; 33 Japanese institutes; 270 non-diabetic vs. 119 diabetic; propensity score matching applied.
**Rehab take:** At MFB IRF, DM status in older cervical SCI patients should directly inform prognosis discussions and realistic recovery goal-setting; endocrine consultation for glycemic optimization during the acute-to-subacute window is a potentially modifiable factor worth pursuing prospectively.

### 3. MEDIUM — Nationwide SCI Cohort Confirms Elevated Long-Term DM Risk Post-Injury
PMID [39337199](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39337199/)
*[Journal]* · 2024 | IM02

**Key outcomes:** Among 6,129 SCI patients and 22,979 matched controls (Korean NHIS 2010–2018), SCI patients showed significantly elevated short- and long-term risk of developing diabetes mellitus; risk modulated by post-SCI disability severity and injury level.
**Rehab take:** IRF admission for new SCI is the ideal window to obtain a baseline HbA1c and initiate DM screening/counseling — the metabolic trajectory post-SCI makes early intervention clinically meaningful even without a prior DM diagnosis.

## IM03 — AKI / Contrast Nephropathy

### 4. MEDIUM — Structured Rehabilitation Associates with Better Renal Recovery After CVD-Related AKI
PMID [38997657](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38997657/)
*BMC Nephrology* · 2024 | IM03

**Key outcomes:** In 107 CVD patients with AKI, structured rehabilitation group (≥1×/week for ≥8 weeks; N=36) showed better renal recovery trajectories vs. controls (N=71); rehab exposure independently associated with improved kidney function outcomes.
**Methods:** Single-center retrospective analysis; CVD patients with AKI (KDIGO criteria); rehab ≥1 session/week for ≥8 weeks; Hamamatsu University Hospital, 2016–2020.
**Rehab take:** For IRF patients admitted post-cardiac event with concurrent AKI history, early structured rehab initiation appears to carry secondary renal benefit — monitor creatinine/GFR trajectory at IRF admission alongside functional gains.

## IM05 — Atrial Fibrillation & Anticoagulation

### 6. ⭐ HIGH — CATALYST: Early DOAC (≤4 Days) Post-Stroke Reduces Events in AF [TOP 3 #1]
PMID [40570866](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40570866/)
*The Lancet* · 2025 | IM05

**Key outcomes:** Composite endpoint (recurrent ischemic stroke, symptomatic ICH, unclassified stroke at 30 days): 2.12% early vs. 3.02% later; OR 0.70 (95% CI 0.50–0.98). Early DOAC superior; no significant increase in symptomatic ICH.
**Methods:** IPD meta-analysis of 4 RCTs (TIMING, ELAN, OPTIMAS, START); N=5,411; early DOAC (≤4 days from stroke onset) vs. later (≥5 days) in ischemic stroke + AF; primary composite endpoint at 30 days.
**Rehab take:** For any AF-stroke patient arriving at MFB who is not yet anticoagulated, confirm on day of IRF admission whether ≤4 days have elapsed since stroke — if so, initiate DOAC immediately unless frank hemorrhagic conversion or other documented contraindication is present.

### 7. ⭐ HIGH — 5-RCT Meta-Analysis Corroborates Early AC Safety in Post-Stroke AF (N=6,810)
PMID [41697537](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41697537/)
*[Meta-analysis]* · 2025 | IM05

**Key outcomes:** 5 RCTs, N=6,810; early DOAC initiation was safe and modestly reduced recurrent ischemic stroke without increasing symptomatic ICH or all-cause mortality.
**Methods:** Systematic review and meta-analysis; PubMed, EMBASE, CENTRAL, Scopus through May 2025; early (≤4 days) vs. later (5–14 days) DOAC; primary outcomes: recurrent ischemic stroke, symptomatic ICH, all-cause mortality.
**Rehab take:** Combined with CATALYST above, the evidence base across ~12,000 patients is definitive — IRF admission is the safety-net opportunity to ensure early DOAC is not delayed for AF-stroke patients.

## IM07 — Aspiration Pneumonia

### 9. ⭐ HIGH — Multimodal Prevention Reduces AP Recurrence; Anticholinergics Worsen Swallowing Risk
PMID [40499817](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40499817/)
*Infectious Diseases Now* · 2025 | IM07

**Key outcomes:** Multimodal approach (semi-sitting positioning, speech therapy, rehabilitation, dental biofilm elimination) significantly effective in AP prevention; no single pharmacological agent was significantly effective alone; anticholinergics, sedatives, and psychotropics significantly worsen swallowing.
**Methods:** Systematic review; PubMed + Cochrane Library; January 2000–January 2025; AP recurrence prevention; multidisciplinary (rehab, nutrition, oral hygiene, pharmacology).
**Rehab take:** Medication reconciliation at IRF admission for SCI/stroke patients with dysphagia must flag anticholinergics (oxybutynin, bladder agents), sedatives, and benzodiazepines — drug reduction is a direct AP prevention lever, triggered by the SLP swallowing evaluation.

## IM10 — Polypharmacy / Beers Criteria

### 14. MEDIUM — Medication Complexity and Anticholinergic Burden Predict Fall History in Older Inpatients
PMID [39918732](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39918732/)
*International Journal of Clinical Pharmacy* · 2025 | IM10

**Key outcomes:** N=194 inpatients aged ≥65 (mean age 80.2 years); mean 7.8 regular medications; 55% reported falls in prior 6 months; 24% reported ≥2 falls. MRCI ≥20, AEC ≥2, and elevated Drug Burden Index each independently associated with fall history.
**Rehab take:** At MFB IRF admission, calculating the MRCI and AEC from the preadmission medication list is a rapid, actionable fall-risk screen — scores above threshold should trigger same-day pharmacist review targeting Beers-listed anticholinergics common in SCI patients.
