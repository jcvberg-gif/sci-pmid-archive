🔬 ISSUE THEME: THE 12-HOUR MANDATE AND THE ANTICOAGULANT RECKONING A definitive 22-study meta-analysis closes the operative timing debate: ultra-early decompression (≤12h) doubles neurological recovery odds (OR 2.30, P<0.00001). A Feb 2026 meta-analysis demolishes the UFH default: LMWH cuts VTE mortality 57% with no bleeding penalty. RISCIS riluzole was COVID-truncated but the global statistical test trends positive. ⭐⭐ WEEKLY SPOTLIGHT — TOP 5 THIS CYCLE

1. ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #1 — Ultra-Early Decompression (≤12h) Doubles Recovery Odds

PMID 41036037  |  Surgical Neurology International · 2025 A01
Key outcomes: ≤12h vs >12h: OR 2.30 (95%CI 1.69–3.14, P<0.00001). ≤24h vs >24h: OR 1.49. No increased mortality or complications.
Methods: Systematic review + meta-analysis; 22 studies; N=2,395 (346 ≤12h · 1,140 ≤24h · 908 >24h).
Surgical/ICU take: OR 2.30 is operationally decisive. 24h AANS/CNS threshold is the floor, not the ceiling. Cervical injuries: mobilize OR within 6–8h. Notify spine surgery from trauma bay. MAP, airway, and operative timing are a parallel process, not serial.

2. ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #2 — LMWH Beats UFH in Spine Trauma VTE — 57% Mortality Reduction, No Bleeding Penalty

PMID 40071770  |  2026 A07
Key outcomes: AC vs none: DVT OR 0.40. LMWH vs UFH: DVT OR 0.78; PE OR 0.66; mortality OR 0.43 (P<0.0001). Major bleeding OR 0.52 (P=0.14, NS).
Methods: SR + meta-analysis; 16 studies (10 retrospective + 6 prospective); spine trauma.
Surgical/ICU take: Switch to LMWH now. Enoxaparin 30mg BID or 40mg daily (renal-dose). Initiate within 72h per Consortium SCI Medicine 3rd Ed. SCDs bridge perioperative window. UFH default has no evidence basis in spine trauma.

3. ⭐⭐ TOP 3 #3 — RISCIS Riluzole: COVID-Truncated, Underpowered, Global Stat Positive — Neuroprotection Not Dead

PMID 40712181  |  PMC12309938 · 2025 A04
Key outcomes: UEMS +1.76 at 180d (95%CI −2.54 to +6.06, NS). Global stat (UEMS+SCIM+SF-36 PCS): positive trend. No drug-related SAEs.
Methods: Phase III RCT; cervical AIS A–C, C4–C8, ≤12h; riluzole 100mg BID→50mg BID×13d; N=193 (54.9% of 351 planned); halted COVID-19 May 2020.
Surgical/ICU take: Interpret NS as inconclusive, not negative — 45% underpowered. Global stat positive. Compassionate use: riluzole within 12h for AIS A–C cervical, no contraindications. 100mg NG q12h×24h → 50mg q12h×13d.

4. ⭐ HIGH — The Phenylephrine Problem: 57% of SCI Vasopressor Use — Wrong Agent — N=3,636

PMID 40693862  |  Critical Care Medicine · October 2025 A06
Key outcomes: N=3,636 SCI T8+, vasopressor ≤48h: phenylephrine 57%, NE 15%, multiple 25%, other 3%. NE more frequent in higher severity.
Surgical/ICU take: Default to norepinephrine for neurogenic hypotension in cervical SCI. Phenylephrine causes reflex bradycardia, compounding unopposed vagal tone. Avoid dopamine. Target MAP 75–90 mmHg.

5. ⭐ HIGH — Multimodal Respiratory Weaning Bundle (MRWI) Improves Vent Liberation in Cervical SCI

PMID 41062804  |  Spinal Cord · 2025;63(11):579–587 A13 A14
Key outcomes: MRWI improved vent liberation at ICU discharge. Birmingham NHS QI Jan 2022–Jan 2025.
Methods: Retrospective baseline + prospective QI. Bundle: (1) readiness-to-wean proforma, (2) RISCI guidelines, (3) weekly MDT SCI round.
Surgical/ICU take: C1–C4: trach ≤7d. C5–C8: daily diaphragm U/S, daily SBT, NIF ≥−20 cmH₂O. Weekly SCI MDT round = lowest-barrier structural intervention. PM&R consult ICU day 1.

6. ⭐ HIGH — Precision MAP: ≥85 Fails AIS Conversion — Sweet Spot 76–104 mmHg (First 48h)

PMID 40922801 · PMC12414179 · 2025 A02
Key outcomes: EAST N=164: MAP ≥85 for 96h failed to increase AIS conversion. Sweet spot 76–104 mmHg first 48h. SCPP 60–90 mmHg optimal in 92 catheter-monitored patients.
ICU take: Treat floor ≥75–80 aggressively; ceiling individualized. SCPP monitoring at specialized centers. [Week 23 TOP 3 #1]

PMID 41455816 · Spinal Cord · Dec 27, 2025 A01
ICU take: QoL also benefits from ultra-early surgery. Family counseling: "not just more movement — better quality of life."

8. MEDIUM — Autonomic Dysfunction Post-SCI: Cardiovascular Instability Independent of AIS Level

PMC12815724 · Frontiers Neurology · Jan 6, 2026 A02 A06
ICU take: AIS grade ≠ autonomic risk. Continuous cardiac monitoring for ALL SCI patients regardless of completeness.

9. MEDIUM — AO Spine 2024-Informed Clinical Recommendations (Dtsch Ärztebl Int Oct 2025)

PMC12693672 · Deutsches Ärzteblatt Int · October 2025 A03 A15
ICU take: MPSS ≤8h only, no TBI, no contraindications — that window is usually missed in polytrauma. Decision must happen in the trauma bay.

10. MEDIUM — AS Cervical Fractures: 858 Patients — Posterior vs Combined Approaches (Cureus Dec 2025)

PMC12831493 · Cureus · Dec 24, 2025 A08
ICU take: AS = ground-level fall = potential 3-column fracture. Alert anesthesia at trauma bay: awake fiberoptic intubation.

11. MEDIUM — C-Spine Clearance Updated Meta-Analysis: CT Clears 99.3%, MRI Changes 4.9%

PMID 38266326 · 2024 A09
ICU take: CT-based clearance safe for 99.3%. MRI still indicated for deficit inconsistent with CT, SCIWORA-risk, or equivocal CT. Coordinate with neurosurgery in ambiguous cases.

12. MEDIUM — Spinal GSW Systematic Review: Surgery Doesn't Improve Complete SCI — No Orthosis (NASJ 2025)

PMID 40756246 · NASJ · Jun 21, 2025 A10
ICU take: No collar, no TLSO. Operate only for progressive deficit, intradural bullet, CSF leak, vascular injury, incomplete compression. Steroids absolutely contraindicated in penetrating SCI.

13. MEDIUM — Pediatric SCIWORA Redefined: 60 Studies, 848 Patients (J Clin Med Sept 2025)

PMID 40944097 · J Clin Med · Sept 2025 A11
ICU take: Peds + neurological deficit + normal CT = MRI mandatory. Immobilize until MRI. PM&R consult ICU day 1.

14. MEDIUM — AIS Grade E Conversion: Median 171 Days — Full FI Only 14.3% If Age >70 (EMSCI N=4,286)

PMID 40873959 · 2025 A12
ICU take: AIS E ≠ full recovery in older patients. Use SCIM III at discharge. Outpatient PM&R at 3 and 6 months mandatory for elderly AIS E.

15. MEDIUM — SCI ICU Outcomes N=341: AIS Grade, Age, Surgical Timing Predict Recovery (PLOS ONE May 2025)

PMC12121827 · PLOS ONE · May 29, 2025 A13
ICU take: Admission AIS + age + hours-to-surgery = 3-factor prognostic tool for day-3 family meeting. Single-center China; apply directionally.

16. MEDIUM — Surgical Timing Narrative Review: ≤24h = Threefold AIS Improvement Chance

PMID 41220893 · PMC12599412 · 2025 A01
ICU take: Accessible narrative companion to meta-analyses. Effective for family counseling on operative urgency.

17. LOW — Pharmacologic Landscape: MPSS History, Riluzole Evidence, Minocycline Pipeline

PMID 41220890 · PMC12599439 · 2025 A03 A04 A15
